Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

SCP Archives Uploader ("the app") is a personal desktop tool used only by its creator, on accounts the creator owns.

  • What the app accesses: the basic profile (display name) of the connected TikTok account, and permission to upload videos to it. The same applies to the connected YouTube channel.
  • Where data is stored: login tokens are stored only on the creator's own computer. Nothing is sent to any server operated by the app.
  • Sharing: the app does not collect, sell or share data about any other person.
  • Removal: access can be revoked at any time in the TikTok and Google account settings.
